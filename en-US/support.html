Help

How to seal a draw, how to check one, and answers to common questions.

Help Privacy

Raffle draws numbers from a range and names from a list, shuffles a list into an order and deals it into teams. What it adds to an ordinary draw is the sealed draw: you seal the draw before it happens, and afterwards anyone can check that the result was not chosen by hand. Everything works offline and without an account.

A sealed draw, in four steps

Under the Draw button, Seal this draw fixes the range or the list and the rules (winners and reserves per round, or the number of teams) and shows a seal of twelve characters, such as K7QF-2M9X-TR4B. Sealing and checking are free.

  1. Show the seal first. Read it out, hold the phone up or tap Send the seal. Nothing has been drawn yet.
  2. Take a word from the room. Ask someone else for any word or number, type it under Word from the room and tap Use this word. It is mixed into the draw. You can draw without a word, but then the certificate shows less (see below).
  3. Draw. The result is marked with a small seal. Certificate opens the record of the draw; Send the certificate shares it as plain text.
  4. Anyone checks. In Raffle, open the menu (top right) or Settings and choose Check a draw, then paste the certificate and tap Check. The app works out the seal and the result again and answers It holds or It does not hold. The last step is yours: compare the seal with the one you were shown before the draw.

While a draw is sealed, the list, the range and the rules cannot be changed; editing asks first and takes the seal off. Numbers and names are drawn round after round under one seal; a sealed order and sealed teams are drawn once. Skip earlier winners does not apply to a sealed draw: its pool is exactly what the seal covers.

What a certificate proves, and what it cannot

If the seal was shown before the word was chosen, and someone other than the organiser chose the word, nobody could know or steer the result: the organiser did not know the word when sealing, and the others did not know the key. Without a word, a certificate only shows that the list, the rules and the result were not changed after sealing. Raffle cannot tell who chose the word or when the seal was shown; the people present can.

A certificate contains the whole list it was drawn from. Send it only to people who may see those names.

Checking by hand: the recipe

A certificate carries everything needed: the key (32 hex digits), the word, the rules, and the range or every entry. Version 1 of the recipe:

  • Seal: SHA-256 over these lines joined by line feeds: raffle-seal-v1, the key, the kind (numbers, names, order, teams), winners=W, reserves=R, teams=T, then range=A..B or one line per entry: tickets, a tab, the name. Each of the first twelve bytes b becomes the character at position b mod 32 of ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789.
  • Random values: block i of round r (both from 0) is HMAC-SHA256 with the key bytes over raffle-draw-v1, the word, r and i, joined by line feeds; each block is cut into four big-endian 64-bit values. An index below n: take the next value v; if v is below 264 mod n take another; otherwise the index is v mod n.
  • Numbers: list the numbers not drawn yet in ascending order; for pick i (from 0) swap position i with position i + index(below what is left from i); the picks are the first positions. Ranges above 200,000 numbers: lower bound + index(below the count), taken again if already drawn.
  • Names: a ticket index below the tickets still in play; walk the list subtracting each entry’s tickets until it fits; that entry comes out with all its tickets.
  • Order: the same swaps as for numbers over the whole list. Teams: that order dealt round-robin into the teams.

Everyday draws

  • Numbers: tap the range (1 – 100 at first) to change it, then tap Draw. Each number comes out only once until you tap New draw.
  • Names: pick Names among the chips under the title, tap Add names and type or paste one name per line. Numbering such as “1.” or “2)” and dashes from a chat are removed, and a name that appears twice counts once. Tap Done, then Draw.
  • Order puts the whole list in one random order, first to last. Teams deals the list into two to twenty teams of even size; set the count under the Draw button.
  • Show to the room (the arrows at the top right) puts the result full screen on a dark ground, with Draw at the bottom for the next one.
  • Everything drawn so far stays on screen, in order, until you start a new draw. Each result has its date, its time and a six-letter draw code worked out from the time and the contents: two cards with the same code show the same draw, and a card whose list was edited afterwards no longer matches.

Tickets: more chances for one name

In the name list, end a line with x3 (after a space) to give that name three tickets in a names draw, from 2 to 99. A name with more tickets is more likely to come out, and still comes out only once. Order, Teams and Secret pairs count everyone once.

Secret pairs

With Raffle Pro, choose Secret pairs and tap Draw the pairs: everyone on the list draws someone else, and nobody draws themselves. Then pass the phone round. Each person taps their own name, taps Show my match, reads it, and hides it again before passing the phone on. The list shows who has looked. The organiser can open every pair from the menu (the app warns first) or draw again. The pairs stay on the phone until a new draw replaces them and never enter the history.

Before drawing, Keep two people apart adds a rule that two people never draw each other, in either direction: partners, siblings, last year’s match. If the rules leave no way to pair everyone, the app says so and asks you to remove one.

Questions

Is the draw really random?

An ordinary draw uses the system’s cryptographically secure random number generator, on your device, so each number or name still in the draw has exactly the same chance. A sealed draw takes its values from the recipe above: a key made by that same generator, mixed with the word from the room. The app never uses the time of day, your taps or anything else to pick.

Can I turn the cage animation or the sounds off?

Yes: in Settings (the gear, top left) switch off the animation or Sound and haptics. The animation switch is also in the options under the Draw button. With Reduce Motion on, results appear at once, and the silent switch always wins over the sounds.

What are reserves?

With Raffle Pro one tap can draw several winners and then reserves, all different. If a winner cannot take part, the reserves step in, in the order they were drawn.

What does “Skip earlier winners” do?

Whoever won an earlier draw from the same saved list, or from the same number range, stays out of the next draw. Reserves are not counted as winners, and a list that has not been saved is never compared with other lists. When everyone has already won, the app says so and offers to include earlier winners again.

What is the number board?

A grid of 10 to 1000 numbers (starting at 1 or at 00). Tap a number to note who has it; tap Draw among taken numbers to draw a winner. A number that has been drawn does not take part again, so you can draw several numbers in turn. Deleting the history does not change the board. The board is only a record: the app has no prices, handles no money and awards no prizes. If there are prizes, follow the law in your country.

What is free, and what is Raffle Pro?

Free: number draws from any range, name draws from a list (with tickets), order, teams, sealed draws with their certificate, checking any certificate, the cage, the room view and the draw so far. Raffle Pro (one payment, no subscription) adds several winners and reserves in one tap, skipping earlier winners, saved lists, secret pairs, the number board, the history with date, time and code, and sharing results as an image or PDF (the board as an image). Options that need Pro show a PRO mark; the Board and History tabs show a sample and a Raffle Pro button until then.

I paid but it is still locked

Open Settings (the gear, top left) and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does Raffle collect?

None. The privacy page lists what stays on your device and the few moments the app goes online.
