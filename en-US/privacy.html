Privacy

What Raffle keeps, where it keeps it, and what it never sends.

Help Privacy

Raffle: Random Number Draw is made to run a draw in front of people without sending anything about them anywhere. This page covers the app only (bundle app.klarwerk.raffle), as of the date at the bottom.

In short

Account or sign-inNone. The app never asks for a name, an email address or a phone number.
PermissionsNone. No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or notifications.
Analytics, ads, trackingNone. No third-party code of any kind is built into the app.
Where your lists liveOn your device only.
When it goes onlineOnly for Apple’s in-app purchase (StoreKit), and when you open a link to these pages.

What stays on your device

The names and ranges you enter, the results drawn so far, saved lists, secret pairs, number boards, the history and the keys of sealed draws are kept in one file inside the app’s own storage. A few small values (the last mode and tab you used, a count of finished draws, and whether Raffle Pro is unlocked) are kept in the app’s preferences. Nothing of this is sent to the developer or to any server. Like the rest of your apps’ data, the file can be part of a device backup you have set up yourself (iCloud or a computer).

To remove it: Settings › Delete history clears the history, and deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

What leaves your device, and only when you choose

  • Sharing. A result, a seal, a certificate, an image or a PDF leaves the app only through the system share sheet or the clipboard, when you tap to send or copy it, and only to the place you pick. A certificate contains the whole list it was drawn from.
  • Pasting. When you check a draw, the app reads the clipboard only when you tap the system Paste button.
  • Purchases. Raffle Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app learns only whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account; payment details stay with Apple.
  • Ratings. After a few finished draws the app may ask iOS to show Apple’s standard rating prompt. iOS decides whether it appears; any rating goes to Apple.
  • These pages. The Help and Privacy links open in your browser. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the request; the developer does not see who opened them.

Children

Raffle is used in classrooms. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either; there is no chat, no profile and no way for the app to contact anyone.
